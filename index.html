Frequently Asked Questions

Good questions. Clear answers.

Still have a question? Get in touch and we can talk through your project.

How much does it cost to build a website?

Website development starts at Rp750,000 for a Landing Page, Rp1,990,000 for a Multi-Page Website, and Rp2,890,000 for a Professional Business Website. Final pricing depends on project scope and complexity.

What is the difference between a Landing Page and Multiple Landing Pages?

A Landing Page is one webpage focused on one goal. Multiple Landing Pages are standalone pages designed for different products, services, campaigns, or audiences.

What is a Company Profile Website?

A Company Profile Website is a multi-page website presenting a business’s identity, background, services, portfolio, and contact information.

How long does website development take?

Timing depends on project complexity, page count, required features, and how quickly materials and feedback are provided. We will discuss an estimated timeline before work begins.

Are revisions included?

Yes. Every package includes up to two rounds of minor revisions. Additional rounds or major changes outside the agreed scope may incur additional charges.

Can I request additional features?

Yes. We will review requests outside the original scope and communicate any additional cost for your approval before proceeding.

Will my website work on smartphones?

Yes. Responsive design is included, with layouts designed for smartphones, tablets, and desktop screens.

Can I request a website redesign?

Yes. Website redesign is one of Nuvora’s services for businesses looking to improve their digital presence.

How do I start a project?

Contact Nuvora through WhatsApp or email with your business information, project requirements, and goals. We will discuss a suitable service and provide an estimate.

Does the starting price include domain and hosting?

Domain and hosting requirements depend on project scope. They are not assumed to be included in every package and will be discussed before work begins.
